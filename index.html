        Check it with your lead line

          How to take a good check
          • Pick a charted spot depth on a flat, even bottom, away from drop-offs, so being a boat-length off the spot doesn't matter.
          • Stop the boat. Lower the lead until the line goes slack, then lift until it just touches, with the line straight up and down.
          • Read the mark at the water surface, not at the deck or the gunwale.
          • Take three casts and enter the middle one. Soft mud and weed let the lead sink, so a firm bottom gives a truer reading.
          • Choose a calm spell: wind piles water up at the downwind end of a lake.
          • Check the rope's marks against a tape now and then. Rope stretches and shrinks when it's wet.

            Official reports

              Waterways Ireland's report lists each lock's ordinary summer level and the 9:00 reading, published by 9:30 on weekdays (weekend readings appear on Monday). Its levels and the ESB's are in Poolbeg datum: take off 2.71 m to compare them with this page. Ardnacrusha uses about 100 m³/s per turbine, so around 400 means all four are running and Lough Derg is being drawn hard.
